How we know

Trueup's offline lab found 32 confirmed trace disagreements and has five locally proven patches. Every patch is a proposal until its upstream project merges it. The build history is preserved by Entire; the business model is in cfo.ai.

The 737 MAX was intended to show an AOA DISAGREE message when two sensor readings differed. Boeing says a software error made it inoperative on most aircraft; the return-to-service update enabled it across the fleet. This analogy does not claim the alert's absence caused either accident. The two accidents killed 346 people.
