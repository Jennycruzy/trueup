What Trueup is (README first line, word for word):
"Trueup finds where your AI agent's traces disagree with what really happened, fixes the tracing code, and proves the fix."

Plain idea: teams watch their AI agents through tracing libraries (neatlogs, OpenInference, OpenLLMetry). These wrap the AI provider's SDK and record each call. When the wrapper gets a case wrong, the dashboard shows a crashed run as OK, misses a refusal, or shows double the real cost. Trueup runs the agent against a fake provider (truth), captures what the trace says, compares, and when they disagree finds the cause in the tracing library, writes a fix with a test, and proves it. Workers: planner, runner, comparer, checker, deduper, fixer, verifier, reporter. The language model proposes; code decides.
